---
title: "New Admin"
description: "Setting up a new Corto admin"
---

This guide is for the initial signup of a new Corto admin.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Get the user to sign up to the [Developer Console Admin](https://admindeveloperconsole.dev.docorto.ai/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify email">
    Verify email as per the signup flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reach out to the Corto Backend team">
    Either Slack or email the Corto Backend team to review and validate the User.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Corto Backend team to action">
    The Corto Backend team will set your Auth0 User's metadata to Active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access admin site">
    You now have access to the admin site.
  </Step>
</Steps>
